OBSERVATORY GUIDE

Drag — orbit camera   Scroll / pinch — zoom

Click / tap an object — select & inspect, with velocity vector, orbit path, and sphere of influence shown

Right-click / long-press empty space, or the Create Object button — open the spawn menu

While placing an object: click for an automatic stable orbit, or click-drag to aim and set its exact launch velocity — a live predicted path is shown, so you can engineer precise gravitational slingshots before releasing.

Moons need a planet selected first — they'll be launched into orbit around it.

Select a star and choose Enter Star System to smoothly fly in and frame its planets and moons. Use Follow / Auto-Orbit to keep the camera locked on any selected object, or Black Hole View to fly back out. The bar under the header shows your current position in the hierarchy.

Objects that stray too close to a black hole fragment, spiral in, and briefly flare the accretion disk as they're consumed.

Two planets, moons, stars, or comets that touch will either merge (a slow, gentle encounter) or shatter into debris (a fast, violent one) — try steering one into another with the drag-launch tool.

The ⚙ icon opens a Physics Debug HUD with live FPS/timing stats and toggleable overlays: velocity, force, and acceleration vectors on every body, the system's center of mass, and each object's collision radius.

Time runs from 0.1x to 1000x — speed it up to watch stars age: they swell into red giants (or supergiants), then quietly fade to a white dwarf, or for massive stars, go supernova and collapse into a neutron star or a brand new black hole.

Two black holes that drift close will orbit each other, slowly decay, and eventually merge into one larger black hole — with a rippling gravitational-wave shockwave through the whole scene.

Generate New Universe builds a fresh, randomized system from scratch. Save / Load keep a snapshot in this browser; Export downloads it as a .json file you can keep or share.

The Object Browser (top of the right panel) lists everything currently in the sim — click any entry to select it and fly there. Log entries marked with ↗ are clickable too, jumping you straight to where that event happened.
